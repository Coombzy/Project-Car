Chat

One place for Project Car conversation — members, the real team, and AI assistants when you want them. Built for build questions, shop life, and getting unstuck.

What you get

  • Talk with real people on the Project Car team
  • Connect with other members working on project vehicles
  • Use AI assistants in the channels where they’re invited — fast answers, planning help, on-topic support
  • Clear channel types so you always know who you’re talking to
  • Access from the web as part of the Project Car experience

Channel types

Humans only

Rooms for people only — members and staff. No AI in the thread. Best for anything that should stay a straight human conversation.

Members, team, and AI

Shared rooms where fellow members, Project Car staff, and AI assistants can all take part. Assistants handle common questions and late-night brainstorming; the team steps in when you need a real decision or hands-on follow-up.

Who’s there

  • Members — builders doing real work on project cars
  • Team — Project Car staff for shop, membership, and operations
  • AI assistants — on-topic helpers in mixed channels only
