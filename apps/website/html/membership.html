Membership

Project Car is a 24/7 community-driven automotive maker-space for project vehicles. Membership is how people get access to the shop, bays, tools, and community.

What membership is for

Membership is aimed at people doing real mechanical and fabrication work on custom and project cars — not casual tire storage. If you need space to tear down and rebuild, tools beyond a home garage, and a practical environment to plan and finish a build, this model is for you.

It is also for builders who want more than an empty bay: shared shop capability, people who understand project cars, and support when a job needs a second opinion or a clearer plan.

What’s included (intended model)

This is the direction we are building toward. Exact packaging will firm up as the shop comes online — nothing below is a live open offer yet.

  • 24/7 shop access (when operational)
  • Individual work bays with 2-post hoists
  • Toolbox and work table per bay
  • Access to shared fabrication tools and shop equipment
  • Community expertise and project support
  • Project planning and mechanical, electrical, and fabrication mentorship
  • Member access to the primary shop camera feed (future)

How it works

At a high level, interested builders join as members, get bay and shop access under shared safety and house rules, and use the space for active project work. Bay allocation, onboarding, and day-to-day rules will be defined properly before a full open program — we are not inventing fine print on this page.

If you are interested now, the most useful step is to reach out. Early interest helps us size demand, prioritize equipment, and shape membership so it stays fair and workable when doors open.

Bay schedule & fair access

Access is meant to stay fair as the shop fills up. The direction is a simple weekly bay schedule: each bay shows who is booked and when, so members can plan wrench time without guessing whether a hoist is free. Blocks can reflect shared sessions or dedicated windows depending on how a project is running.

Over time, that schedule pairs with a lightweight token-style allowance so heavy users do not quietly crowd out everyone else — think clear claims on bay time, not a maze of rules.
