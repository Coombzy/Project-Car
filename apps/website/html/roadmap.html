Roadmap

Where Project Car is headed. This will evolve as the shop and systems come online.

Now

  • Public site live: Home, About, The Shop, Membership, Roadmap, Contact
  • Public waitlist on Membership / Contact (Shop API)
  • Core shop concept defined: 24/7-oriented access, individual bays, real tools, project-focused
  • About page documents full value stack (space, tools, mentorship, parts/delivery, member app)
  • Membership model being shaped from real interest and feedback

Next

  • Physical shop build-out toward the multi-bay model
  • Clearer membership access details as the space comes together
  • Member app basics: bay schedule, profiles/onboarding, shop chat
  • Parts & materials flow — order support and delivery/staging to the shop and bays
  • YouTube channel to document the build of the business, our project cars, and member builds

Later

  • Live primary shop camera for members
  • Deeper member tools (project notes, order tracking, ops support)
  • Expanded fabrication capability
  • Community programs and mentorship in practice
  • Ongoing video documentation of shop progress and real project work

Early build. Priorities will shift as the shop and community take shape.
