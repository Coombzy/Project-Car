The Shop

A community-driven automotive maker space built around real project work — space to build, tools to fabricate, and people who get it.

Project Car is designed as a maker-space style shop for custom mechanical and fabrication work on project vehicles. The goal is straightforward: give builders the room, equipment, and a floor run to shop standards — so plans can turn into finished cars.

What’s planned for the floor

This is the intended shop capability — not a claim that every bay or tool is live yet. Access packaging lives on Membership.

  • Individual work bays with 2-post hoists and room to stage a project
  • Shared fabrication floor for mechanical and metalwork
  • Safety, cleanliness, and tool-care standards
  • A structured shop — not unsupervised storage

How the shop will be managed

Project Car is being built as a structured maker-space, not an unsupervised storage unit with a toolbox in the corner. Builders work in individual bays so projects stay contained and respectful of shared floorspace. Clear expectations for safety, cleanliness, and tool care keep the floor usable for everyone.

Day to day, the value is in more than square footage. Shared fabrication capability and hoists in the bays are part of the model. Community standards and guided use of equipment help keep quality high without turning the place into a red-tape warehouse.

Why not just a rental bay or the home garage?

A home garage is convenient until the job needs a hoist, real fab capacity, or space you do not want filled with dust, noise, and half-finished assemblies for months. Many home setups also hit limits on power, ventilation, neighbor tolerance, and simply having the right tool when you need it.

A plain rented unit can solve storage, but it rarely solves capability. You still haul tools, hunt for specialty equipment, and work around missing shop infrastructure. Project Car’s direction is to combine dedicated bay space with a shared fabrication floor and standards that keep the place usable — so builders spend more time building and less time improvising around a home garage or empty unit.

The shop concept is in early build. Features and operating details will expand here as systems and facilities come online.
