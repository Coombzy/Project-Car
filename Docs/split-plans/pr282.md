### 2. Cancel window, no-show, and a started visit — [D5 · S4 C4 R4 T2]

- **Files from `1302f0b`:** `apps/project-car/api/tests/test_cancel_policy.py`, `apps/project-car/api/tests/test_started_visit.py`, and the cancel, sweep, and settle functions in `bookings.py`.
